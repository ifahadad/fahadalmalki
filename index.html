About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Linguistics at Rutgers University.

My research focuses on theoretical linguistics, with particular interests in syntax, semantics, morphology, and the syntax-morphology interface.

I am especially interested in Arabic and Semitic languages and in questions concerning the architecture of the grammar.
