Research

My research investigates the architecture of grammar and the interaction between syntax, semantics, morphology, and the lexicon. I am particularly interested in how cross-linguistic variation informs theories of grammatical structure.

Syntax

My work in syntax focuses on generative approaches to locality, binding, anaphora, argument structure, movement, case, and the syntax of subjects and objects.

Morphology

I am interested in the relationship between morphology and syntax, with particular attention to Distributed Morphology, agreement, number, allomorphy, and non-concatenative morphology.

Semantics

My semantic interests include reference, anaphora, indexicality, interpretation, and the relationship between syntactic structure and semantic composition.

Arabic & Semitic Linguistics

A substantial part of my research examines Arabic and other Semitic languages. I use data from these languages to address broader theoretical questions in syntax, morphology, and morphosyntax.
